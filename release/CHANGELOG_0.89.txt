World Builder 0.89

Fixed
- Previews seemed not to render at all after a fresh start or after deleting the thumbs folder: the index starts with
  about 17,800 prefabs that have no static mesh (characters with skinned meshes, decals, effects), and the preview worker
  read every one of them before reaching the first object - more than five minutes without a single image. They are
  now recognised from the prefab index and skipped without being read; the first object previews appear right away.
- The one-time retry of earlier failures in 0.87 therefore no longer spends minutes on those prefabs either.
