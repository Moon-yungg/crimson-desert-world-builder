World Builder 0.81

New (experimental)
- Interactive objects through the game's own spawn path. Page Up spawns a gimmick two meters in front of you the way
  the game spawns its field objects: a stand torch by default, or any prefab path typed into the "replay prefab path
  override" box in the Log tab (the game knows 13,941 gimmick prefabs: torches, lamps, doors, chests, campfires ...).
  A torch spawned this way can be lit and put out like a real one. "remove" in the Log tab's "spawned by replay" list
  takes an object away again, exactly as the game removes a picked-up item.
  The spawn needs a template: the mod copies the parameters of a spawn the game itself made and swaps prefab, position
  and identity. Such spawns happen all the time while you walk (level streaming), so a few steps after loading are
  enough; before the first one Page Up says "walk a few meters first". This is research-grade: not saved in projects
  yet, no rotation yet, no gizmo, and a game update may switch it off (the log then says which hook is missing).

Fixed
- Hooks could fail at startup ("hooks missing" in the editor, MH_ERROR_MEMORY_ALLOC in the log) when no free memory was
  left within 2 GB of the game image. The mod now reserves that space at attach time and hands it out hook by hook.
- The selection outline and the gizmo center follow the game's real render camera (its view and projection matrices are
  located in memory), so they stay on the object when the camera moves or the field of view changes (town vs. field).
  The old camera model is still used for the first seconds until the matrices are found.
- The one-time re-rendering of preview images after an update remembers its progress in cdmodkit\thumbs_pass.txt and
  resumes after a restart instead of starting over.
- A crash now writes the call chain and the registers into the log ([fault] lines), which makes reports much more useful.
- Error codes the game returns are decoded to their names in the log (cdmodkit\errnames.txt).
