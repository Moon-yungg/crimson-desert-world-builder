World Builder 0.92

Fixed
- All previews in the browser could vanish at once: the preview texture cache counted tiles whose image did not exist yet
  but never dropped them, so after scrolling past a few hundred such tiles it threw away every real image in every frame.
  Tiles without an image are dropped first now. (Restarting the game was the workaround.)
- The preview progress stopped 32 short of the total ("48694 / 48726") and never finished: 32 prefabs are stored twice in the
  game's packs. They are counted once, and the prefab list no longer shows them twice.
- The browser filter "meshes only" hid prefabs that are made of other prefabs (sets, presets). They spawn fine and are
  listed now. Character, monster and armour prefabs stay hidden with the filter on: they spawn nothing visible.

Added
- HTTP API: GET /api/camera returns the camera position and the direction the screen looks (see HTTP_API.md).
