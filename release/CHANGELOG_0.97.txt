World Builder 0.97

Added
- Terrain tab (also in the dock): raise, lower and flatten the ground with a brush in the world (radius 2 to 60 m, up to
  5 m per stroke, strokes add up). The ground and its collision change right away, no reload needed: you can walk into
  a dip or onto a hill you just painted. Undo, redo and clear all; strokes are saved with the project and are back as
  soon as the project is autoloaded. Apply (a fast travel away and back) is only needed if a stroke could not be shown live.
- Travel tab: travel to any coordinates or to your own saved spots with the game's own fast travel (a loading screen,
  then you stand there, the world streams in). The first travel of a session takes up to half a minute to prepare.

Added (from dofo7777)
- Managed NPCs: spawned NPCs appear in the Scene tab next to your objects and can be selected, moved, grouped, named,
  noted, switched between normal AI and Hold, undone and redone, and are saved with the project.
- Projects: Load, Reload, Save and Unload; unassigned objects and NPCs can be attached to an existing project.
  Optional real-time auto-save of projects. Older project files still load.
- Longer history (up to 1000 steps), scaling up to 20x, more actions in the right-click menu, a fuller dock mode.
- Optional keyboard placement keys (off by default, configurable in Settings).

Changed
- Home switches between editing and player control (the editor stays visible and transparent). The free camera has its
  own button and can optionally start when the editor opens. In the free camera, Q moves down (Ctrl no longer does).
- NPC formations spawn up to 500 NPCs per click.

Fixed
- Ground probing no longer falls through small holes in the terrain.
- The mouse position is correct in windowed mode.
