World Builder 0.91

Added
- Previews for characters, monsters and armour: skinned meshes (.pac) are read now, with their textures. About 15,000 prefabs
  that had no preview get one. Monsters stored as one prefab show the whole creature; human characters are assembled by the
  game at runtime, so their prefabs show the single parts (body, head, upper body, gloves, ...). They are listed when the
  browser filter "meshes only" is off (they still spawn nothing visible through the editor).
- Previews for prefabs made of other prefabs (sets, presets, spots): the referenced prefabs are rendered in place, so the
  preview shows what spawning the prefab shows. About 1,100 more prefabs get a preview.
- Better previews: dye colours (characters and monsters whose colour comes from the dye mask instead of a texture were plain
  beige), normal maps, specular highlights and glow.
- Settings tab: "preview quality" (base colour / + dye colours / + normal maps / + specular and glow, default the best). Previews
  on screen are always rendered first; a lower level only makes the background pass faster.

Fixed
- Every textured preview used wrong texture coordinates. Tiling materials (wood, stone, metal) still looked plausible, atlas
  textures (cloth, props) turned into noise. All existing previews are rendered again once in the background, the tiles on
  screen first.
- After scrolling quickly through the browser, the tiles you stopped at waited behind every tile you had scrolled past. The
  newest request is rendered first now, and tiles that left the screen drop out of the queue.
- Characters were previewed from behind.
